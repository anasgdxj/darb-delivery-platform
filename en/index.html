The DARB delivery platform

Everyday errands have a better route.

Discover a delivery platform designed to bring local shops, customers and independent couriers together. Availability depends on the active service area.

One platform, different journeys

Order from participating local shops or create an independent delivery request when the service is available in your area. Review the details shown in the app before confirming.

For customers

Browse available shops, select products, check your order summary and follow order status in the app. For independent deliveries, enter pickup and drop-off details and review the available courier or pricing options.

For independent couriers

Eligible bicycle, motorcycle and car couriers can apply through the driver registration flow in the app. Applications may require identity, vehicle and other supporting documents, and are subject to review.

For local shops

Shops can apply to list their business and manage product and order information. Activation depends on verification and operational readiness in the service area.

Where is DARB available?

DARB is currently available in Aleppo, Syria. Expansion to other cities will be announced after local service operations are ready.
