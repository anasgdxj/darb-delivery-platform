DAZREVO Delivery Platform

Everyday errands on a faster urban route.

Discover a unified logistics platform built for local stores, everyday customers, and independent couriers across Syria. Live availability adapts by active city zone.

One platform, unified delivery journeys

Order directly from verified neighborhood stores, submit peer-to-peer parcel requests with driver fare bidding, or draft an order using the DAZREVO AI assistant before reviewing transparent delivery fees.

For customers

Browse active shops in your city, pin pickup and drop-off addresses on the map, compare independent driver offers, pay cash on delivery, and track your courier live with GPS updates.

For independent couriers

Bicycle, motorcycle, and car couriers can apply directly inside the app through the 5-step onboarding wizard. Applications are reviewed carefully prior to account activation.

For local merchants

Merchants can register their store, organize catalogs and inventory levels, manage incoming order workflows step by step, and track real operational sales summaries.

Service coverage

Operations launch city-by-city starting in Aleppo and Damascus, expanding as local merchant and courier networks reach readiness thresholds inside the app.

Get started with DAZREVO

Launch the interactive web app or download the Android APK to explore customer, driver, and merchant modes.
